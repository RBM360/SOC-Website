Campus Minister

Scott Jefcoat

Scott serves as the campus minister for SOC Student Ministry, helping create a welcoming space for students to study, share meals, worship, ask questions, and build friendships throughout each student's journey.

“The SOC is always an open invitation! Drop on by and come in. There are always students at the house catching up on school work, hanging out playing cards or ping pong. Some bring food over and eat during their lunch breaks. They have movie nights all the time, etc.”
