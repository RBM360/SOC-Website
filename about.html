Who we are

SOC is a Christian campus community at Tennessee Tech connected with Sycamore Church of Christ. We create space for students to worship, study Scripture, ask honest questions, take retreats and trips together, and build friendships that make campus feel more like home.

Servants of Christ Campus Ministry was started in the Fall of 2008. Since then, SOC has been a prominent campus ministry on TTU's campus, helping students find community, encouragement, and a place to grow in faith.

Scott Jefcoat has served in the campus evangelist role since 2010, walking alongside students as they learn to follow Jesus on campus and carry that faith into the rest of life.
