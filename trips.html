Trips

Retreats, mission trips, field trips, camps, and off-campus memories from SOC at Tennessee Tech. This page is for the moments that happen beyond the regular weekly rhythm.

Service Mission Trips

Each May, right after the Spring semester ends, SOC takes a service mission trip to help church camps get ready for the summer. The group has traveled all over the Southeast, telling camps, "Make us a list of what all you need done," then jumping into a variety of projects.

These trips help students serve while learning practical skills in physical labor, cooking, teamwork, and ministry.

Alaska Outreach

For the past two summers, SOC has taken students to work with the Soldotna Church of Christ in Alaska, joining their efforts to evangelize and reach out to the community.

Visiting Congregations

We have opportunities during the semester to take our group to visit with area congregations, and our young men lead in their worship. If this would be a benefit to your local church and help us get the word out about our campus ministry, please send us an email and we will line something up.

Retreats

Weekend retreats and overnight trips give students time away for prayer, worship, deeper community, and friendships that keep going after everyone gets back to campus.
